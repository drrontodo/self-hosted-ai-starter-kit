## 1. Structure of the 2026 Framework

| Requirement | Amount |
|---|---|
| **Total CPD** | Minimum **50 hours** per calendar year |
| **Category 1 — Educational Activities** | Minimum **12.5 hours** |
| **Categories 2 + 3 combined** (Reviewing Performance + Measuring Outcomes) | Minimum **25 hours** combined |
| **Category 2 — Reviewing Performance** | Minimum **5 hours** |
| **Category 3 — Measuring Outcomes** | Minimum **5 hours** |

- The remaining ~12.5 hours (50 − 12.5 − 25) can be in any category. Within the 25-hour combined requirement, you choose the mix (RACP's own examples: 5h Cat 2 + 20h Cat 3, or 15h Cat 2 + 10h Cat 3).
- **Mandatory activities (annual):**
  - A **Professional Development Plan (PDP)** — mandatory, claimed in Category 2. RACP recommends doing it early in the year.
  - An **Annual Conversation** — a structured discussion of your practice/development needs with a peer, colleague or employer — mandatory Category 2 activity. RACP states these two mandatory activities are expected to cover the 5-hour Category 2 minimum. (A Regular Practice Review using the RACP professional development review process also satisfies the Annual Conversation requirement.)
  - Minimum **2 activities on cultural safety / addressing health inequities** and **2 activities on ethics and professional behaviour** per year. These can sit in any of Categories 1, 2 or 3 (activity counts, not separate hour quotas).
- The 50 hours is **inclusive** of the mandatory activities.

## 2. What Qualifies Under Each Category — and Where Specific Activities Fit

### Category 1 — Educational Activities (min 12.5h)
Traditional CME: conferences, lectures/seminars/workshops, courses, grand rounds, journal clubs, hospital/medical meetings, reading journal articles, online learning modules, podcasts/audio/video learning, self-assessment programs, literature searches (e.g. Medline).

### Category 2 — Reviewing Performance (min 5h)
Activities where **others appraise your actual work**: PDP creation/maintenance, the Annual Conversation, peer review of medical records/practice, patient feedback, multi-source feedback (MSF), documented case discussion with peers, Regular Practice Review, performance appraisals.

### Category 3 — Measuring Outcomes (min 5h)
Measurement of **health outcomes of your practice**: clinical audit against standards/guidelines, audit against your specialty's EVOLVE "Top-5" low-value-care recommendations, prospective audit of a new drug/therapy, incident reporting/monitoring, mortality & morbidity reviews, comparison of individual/team data with local/institutional/regional datasets, institutional audits (hospital accreditation, immunisation program outcomes), formal research analysing health-outcomes data and preparing publications from it.

## 3. Documentation and Evidence Requirements

- **Recording in MyCPD:** entries record the activity, category and hours; the platform lets you attach evidence and reflections and edit entries later. Reflection notes are supported but no RACP statement was found making per-entry reflection mandatory (reflection is structurally built into the mandatory PDP and Annual Conversation).
- **Acceptable evidence (2026 document):**
  - Acknowledgement-of-participation form signed by a peer or supervisor
  - Letter/statement/email from the organiser/administrator
  - Records of participation/attendance: certificates, confirmation of attendance, official meeting minutes, receipts of payment
  - De-identified proposal/evaluation/review/audit documents
  - Completed annual conversation templates; peer review/feedback records; performance/practice review summaries; documented case-discussion accounts (Cat 2)
  - **Personal calendar/diary entries are NOT accepted — except to verify journal reading**
  - Activities done through the College (SPDP, supervising, examining, committee work, accreditation, College journals) are **internally verified** — no evidence needed
  - Evidence must **clearly demonstrate the hours claimed** for each activity
- **Audit:** RACP audits **5% of eligible CPD participants annually, selected at random**. If selected, acceptable evidence sufficient to meet the minimum annual requirements must be provided by **30 June** (for the prior CPD year). RACP **reports compliance, non-compliance and audit non-compliance to the medical regulators** (Ahpra/MBA in Australia; MCNZ in NZ).
- **Retention:** RACP retains participant CPD records for **up to 10 years**. No RACP-published participant-side retention minimum confirmed; evidence must at least survive the audit window (audit of year N runs to 30 June of year N+1). The MBA CPD registration standard has its own retention expectation.

## 4. "Low-Effort but Legitimate" Activities for a Busy Private-Practice Neurologist

**Category 1 (need 12.5h — easy to over-fill)**
- Journal/article reading and medical news — keep a running reading log (date, source, time, one-line takeaway). The only activity where calendar/diary-style records are accepted as evidence.
- Podcasts/webinars/online modules — save completion certificates/confirmation emails into one folder.
- Conference/society meeting attendance (e.g. ANZAN ASM) — registration receipt or attendance certificate.
- Hospital grand rounds / journal club attendance — organiser email or minutes.
- Literature searches for specific patients (UpToDate/Medline) — log like reading.

**Category 2 (need ≥5h — PDP + Annual Conversation are designed to cover this)**
- PDP (mandatory anyway) — completed RACP template is the evidence.
- Annual Conversation — one structured chat with a trusted colleague using the RACP template.
- Informal peer case discussions formalized — a 3-line documented account (date, peer, case theme, learning) is explicitly acceptable evidence.
- MDT meetings where your cases are discussed — minutes or attendance email.
- Patient feedback review — periodic summary of practice feedback (incl. online reviews) with noted actions.
- Referrer feedback — keep correspondence where GPs/peers comment on your letters or management.

**Category 3 (need ≥5h — usually the pinch point; one small audit solves it)**
- A small clinical audit done anyway: e.g. migraine prophylaxis choices, EMG report turnaround, or prescribing against the specialty's EVOLVE recommendations — de-identified audit spreadsheet + one-page summary.
- Incident reporting/monitoring — de-identified records of incidents logged/reviewed in rooms.
- Practice accreditation activities — accreditation documents.
- M&M or outcome review meetings at any hospital — minutes/attendance.
- Medicolegal report audit — sample of reports reviewed against a checklist/standard, with a short written summary (fits the audit definition; not RACP-named, so keep documentation solid).

General evidence tactic: since calendar entries alone are not acceptable (except reading logs), the lowest-effort compliant habit is (a) one folder for certificates/emails/receipts, (b) a running reading log, (c) 3-line documented accounts for peer discussions, (d) completed RACP templates for PDP/Annual Conversation, (e) one de-identified mini-audit per year.

## 5. Caps and Minimums

- **Minimums (confirmed):** 50h total; 12.5h Cat 1; 25h combined Cat 2+3 with ≥5h in each; 2 cultural-safety and 2 ethics/professional-behaviour activities.
- **Caps:** No per-activity or per-category maximum caps could be confirmed for the current hours-based framework (the pre-2023 credit framework's caps are obsolete). Confirm with MyCPD@racp.edu.au if planning depends on it.

## 6. 2026 vs 2025, and Deadlines

- RACP states explicitly: **"The CPD Framework for 2026 is the same as in 2025."**
- **CPD year:** calendar year, 1 January – 31 December 2026.
- **Recording deadline:** 2026 activities must be recorded in MyCPD by **31 March 2027**.
- **Audit evidence deadline:** participants selected for audit have until **30 June** of the following year.
