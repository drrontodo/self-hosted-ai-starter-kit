Meeting recordings

Upload a recording of a professional meeting (Annual Conversation, peer case discussion, M&M/outcomes meeting, journal club). It is transcribed locally by faster-whisper, then summarised into de-identified minutes and a reflection draft that lands in your Inbox for sign-off. Avoid speaking patient names during the meeting; the summariser strips any identifiers it finds. Audio never leaves the server.
